# Nuventa Cloud demo: narration script

Read at a calm pace; about 3 to 4 minutes in total. One paragraph per slide.

**Slide 1: Everything your school needs to run smarter.**

Welcome to Nuventa Cloud: school management software built for Nigerian schools. In the next few minutes, we'll show you how it brings your school's records, exams, results, attendance and fees together in one place.

**Slide 2: Running a school shouldn't mean drowning in paperwork**

Running a school means a mountain of paperwork. Results take weeks to compile by hand. Student records sit in files and spreadsheets. And parents call again and again about results and fees. Nuventa Cloud is built to take that load off your team.

**Slide 3: Everyone gets the screens they need**

Everyone in your school gets their own login. Administrators run the whole school. Teachers manage their classes, scores and attendance. Students log in to sit computer-based tests. And parents follow their children's progress, from a phone or a computer.

**Slide 4: All your school's records, in one place**

Start with your records. Add every student, class, subject, teacher and parent once. Already have them in a spreadsheet? Upload them in one go. You can have separate admins for your nursery, primary and secondary sections, and promote students at the end of the session.

**Slide 5: Set exams without the retyping**

Setting exams is simpler too. Type a paper directly, or import one you have already written in Word. Keep questions in a question bank to reuse in later terms, then schedule and print your papers.

**Slide 6: Report cards from the scores teachers enter**

When exams are done, teachers enter scores once. Nuventa Cloud works out totals, grades and positions, using your own grading system, and produces report cards complete with remarks and signatures. No more weeks of compiling results by hand.

**Slide 7: CBT, marked automatically**

Want to go further? With computer-based testing, students sit exams on computers, and objective questions are marked automatically. Staff follow every student on a live invigilation board. And if your lab's internet is unreliable, an exam station on your school's own network lets students sit papers with no internet at all. CBT is an optional add-on.

**Slide 8: Know who is in school, every day**

Teachers take daily class attendance, and you can see absences across the whole school. Add the optional Gate Tracker, and each student's arrival is recorded by a card scan at the gate, with an alert sent to their parents.

**Slide 9: Parents stay informed without calling the office**

Parents get their own portal to see their child's attendance, results and fees. A parent with several children sees them all under one login. The school can send email updates free of charge, and SMS alerts are available, charged per message.

**Slide 10: See who has paid, and who still owes**

Set fees for each class, record every payment, and see at a glance who still owes. Parents can pay online through Paystack, straight into your school's own account, and they get a receipt for every payment.

**Slide 11: Your own website and portal**

Your school also gets its own web address, with public pages for admissions, activities and contact, in your own logo and colours. Already have a domain name? You can use that instead.

**Slide 12: Up and running in four steps**

Getting started is straightforward. Register your school online, choose the modules you need, upload your records, and start. You pay per student, per term, or for the whole session, and you only add extras if you want them.

**Slide 13: See it working for your school**

Ready to see it working for your school? Book a free demo at nuventacloud.com/book-demo. Nuventa Cloud. Everything your school needs to run smarter.
