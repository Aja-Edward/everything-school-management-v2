Nuventa Cloud Demo: School Management Software for Nigerian Schools

## Description

See how Nuventa Cloud brings your school's records, exams, results, attendance and fees together in one place, in under 4 minutes.

Nuventa Cloud is school management software built for Nigerian schools: nursery, primary and secondary. Teachers enter scores once and report cards are produced for you. Parents follow their children's progress from a phone, and fees are paid online straight into your school's own account.

✔ Exams typed directly or imported from Word, with a reusable question bank
✔ Automatic totals, grades, positions and report cards, using your own grading system
✔ Daily attendance for every class
✔ Parent portal for attendance, results and fees
✔ Fee tracking and online payment through Paystack, with a receipt for every payment
✔ Your own school website with admissions and contact pages

Pricing is per student, per term or for the whole session. You only pay for add-ons if you want them.
